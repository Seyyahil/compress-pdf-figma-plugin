Compress PDF bundles these open-source libraries:

- pdf-lib 1.17.1 — MIT — https://github.com/Hopding/pdf-lib
- pako 2.1.0 — MIT and Zlib — https://github.com/nodeca/pako

Their license notices are retained in the compiled JavaScript. Build and test tools are development dependencies only. Complete license texts are also included in the packaged licenses directory.
